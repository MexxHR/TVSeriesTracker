## Measurement implementation

`official-data/automation/provider_coverage_audit.py` imports existing production discovery, monitored parsing and promotion evidence replay. Fresh TMDB identity/network metadata is used only for identity and routing. The harness does not derive gold lifecycle facts from TMDB. It measures an unregistered identity with empty temporary trusted/monitored registries to exercise generic discovery, even where a title already exists in production. Only the empty audit registry is accepted by the audit wrapper; existing nonempty trusted validation remains intact. This explicit audit fixture does not change stored production data or deployed behavior.

Generic coverage requires finding the specific gold article, followed by identity acceptance, lifecycle facts and independent reconstruction matching the known gold fields. A different announcement for the same series does not count. Direct-URL diagnostic uses a separate cache and explicit diagnostic-only candidate; its parser/reconstruction result never increases generic E2E. Negative evidence is tested against the negative target/article itself. A fetched page or discovered link alone is not COVERED.

Metrics include discovery, raw E2E, accessible E2E, direct parser/replay, false-positive count/rate, stage funnels, lifecycle classes, production-only/all-audited weighted and macro averages, confidence, and rating. Null denominators render N/A. FX never contributes to production metrics. Small samples and source/discovery limits prevent broad ratings. Per-case requests, provider budgets, candidates, truncation, HTTP failures, timeout counts and environment/start/end metadata are reported.

Non-covered eligible cases use the required earliest-stage primary taxonomy; later diagnostic issues are secondary. HTTP403 remains source inaccessibility; HTTP404 invalidates unavailable gold, and uncertain network/identity conditions are excluded or inconclusive rather than fabricated provider failures. WBD access remains separately visible; no bypass or browser automation is used.

ROI and cross-provider ranking are deterministic, transparent and safety-first. Coverage ROI = accessible uncovered cases × dominant failure share; LOW-confidence coverage targets are withheld. Any observed false positive receives higher safety priority (1000 + observed count), including in a small sample. False positives outrank recall gaps; source limitations are separated from potential parser/discovery quick wins. The generated report provides up to three supported investigation targets with measured impact, risk and upper-bound potential gain. Small or inconclusive samples can leave recommendations empty. **No reliable TOP3 is available locally; it is deferred to actual runner evidence.** No V2.7.15 change is implemented.
