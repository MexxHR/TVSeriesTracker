## 7. Spanish parser changes

New pure opt-in module `spanish_qualification_parser.py` accepts only explicit Spanish/ES context. It normalizes Unicode punctuation, whitespace and numeric ordinal markers while retaining accents. It splits bounded editorial text into local clauses and returns evidence snippets of at most 200 characters. Claims require the exact requested title or an explicit configured official alias. No fuzzy translation or article-wide bag-of-words binding is used.

Existing shared helpers receive optional arguments: `provider_audit.fetch_url(..., decoder=None)` permits strict decoding for this qualification; `promotion._validate_evidence(..., detector=None, aliases=None)` permits Spanish excerpt replay. Existing defaults remain the existing English decoder/parser and alias policy. No promotion writer is called. `update.py`, production registries, backend routing and Android behavior remain unchanged.

## 8. Ordinals

Supports primera through décima, including accented séptima/décima, numeric `3ª`, `3.ª`, `3 temporada` and `temporada 3`. Numeric seasons remain bounded to 1–99. No season is borrowed from another clause or title. Dedicated ordinal and Unicode tests cover these forms.

## 9. Renewal

Recognizes title-bound active `renueva`/`ha renovado` with `por` or `para`, passive `renovada`/`renovado`, `ha sido renovada`/`ha sido renovado`, and `se renueva`, with an explicit local season. Bounded series-description/apposition forms support first-party press wording without title-specific parsing rules. Premiere, return, filming and trailer statements alone do not renew a series.

## 10. Final season

Recognizes title-bound numbered `quinta y última temporada`, reverse season-before-title constructions, numbered `temporada, que será la última`, and numbered `temporada N final`. An explicit numbered final renewal yields FINAL_SEASON. Episode/trailer/advance wording is not final-season evidence. A numberless final phrase remains a conservative false negative because this fact/replay contract requires an explicit season; no season is invented or recovered from unrelated text.

## 11. Regional dates and publication dates

Spanish full day/month dates are extracted only from the same title + season + premiere/return construction. Diagnostic records carry `REGIONAL_RELEASE_DATE`, `es`, `ES` and a separate regional date. Production-compatible `releaseDate` and `releaseYear` stay null, including apparently global-looking copy in this qualification release.

The year must be explicit or resolve within a forward interval of at most 183 days in the publication date's same calendar year. Year-boundary ambiguity leaves the date/year null; invalid calendar dates and month-only windows are not fabricated. Regional date extraction cannot contaminate another season's lifecycle fact.

Announcement dates use the existing publication metadata extractor or bounded article JSON-LD with matching canonical URL and headline. Conflicting/invalid visible publication metadata is not overridden by JSON-LD. Only article publication dates are used; dateModified and nearby editorial dates are not substitutes.

## 12. Discovery model

Generic discovery starts only from the regional root and sitemap, traverses actual first-party index links and matches title aliases against actual anchor/path text. It records the complete discovery path. Limits: six index pages, depth three, 2,000 inspected links per index, at most seven candidates per case and 84 qualification HTTP calls overall. Existing bounded HTTP/redirect controls remain in use.

Known article URLs are direct regression probes only. They cannot seed or rank generic discovery, and a directly reachable known URL cannot count as a generic discovery pass. Additional research URLs are provenance fields, not crawler seeds.

## 13. Sitemap usage

The sitemap supplies candidate article links, never lifecycle facts. A separately fetched, identity-checked article is required. Root and sitemap URLs are explicitly rejected by the evidence detector. A known URL may count as discovered only if the current index independently links to it.

## 14. Identity validation

Reuse the V2.7.7 strict requested/final/canonical URL and heading validation, official hostname checks, fetched metadata identity and original-brand agreement. A 200 page for another show, a foreign redirect, a bot page or ambiguous provider identity fails. Editorial extraction excludes navigation, scripts, related/latest-news blocks and footer content.

Additional corpus titles with null IDs require a unique exact TMDB search-name match followed by fresh ID/name/network validation; IDs are never guessed. The explicit Spanish Conan title is an official alias, not an inferred translation.
